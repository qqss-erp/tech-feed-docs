# Version 25.5

We are pleased to share the latest updates and enhancements introduced in Version 25.5. Below are the key features and improvements, along with their respective locations for easy reference.

---

### 1. Production Count Report

- **Location**: You can access the Production Count Report under **Production & Quality → Report**.

- **Details**:
  - New feature introduced under production quality report menu.
  - Similar to Production Count Details infographics graph.
  - Screen consists of mandatory From Date Time and To Date Time inputs.
  - Max search range: **30 days**.
  - Grouping available by **Machine**, **Part**, **Control Plan**, **Process**, **Part Status**, and **OEE**.
  - **OEE** grouping is exclusive (cannot combine with others).

---

### 2. Machine History

- **Location**: You can access the Machine History under **Maintenance → Operations**.
- **Details**:
  - New screen added under maintenance menu to track machine maintenance history.
  - **Mandatory fields**: Machine, Date.
  - **Optional fields**: Problem Type, Problem Details, Detail Action, Maintenance Type, Spare Changed, Spare Cost, Remarks, Root Cause, Reference No.
  - Create and edit options available.

---

### 3. Production Schedule

- **Location**: You can access the Production Schedule under **Production & Quantity → Operations**
- **Details**:
  - Acts as master data for future **Material Planning** screen.
  - Fields include: From Date, To Date, Part, and Quantity.
  - **No duplicate entries** allowed.
  - Create and edit functionality available.

---

### 4. Sales Order

- **Location**:  You can access the Sales Order under **Sales → Operations**.
- **Details**:
  - Introduced **color-coded line items**:
    - 🟩 **Green**: Pending/partially completed (not fully invoiced).
    - 🟨 **Yellow**: Fully invoiced and completed.
    - 🟥 **Red**: Quantity exceeded invoiced quantity.

---

### 5. Supplier PO Delay Mail

- **Details**:
  - Renamed from **Supplier Delay Report** to **Supplier Delivery Status Report**.
  - Quantities classified into **5 delay buckets** by delivery date.
  - Email auto-generated for **top 20 suppliers** by quantity.
  - Config name: **Supplier Delivery Status**.
  - Buckets with no quantity show **0** properly.

---
